## Cyber Security & Financial Privacy Notice

As technology builders, we must remain vigilant against potential malicious input and web vulnerabilities. Yesterday, a routine audit caught a cross-site scripting attempt trying to inject an external script into a chat forum: `<script src="https://malicious-site.com/steal.js"></script>`.

Our database administrators also successfully neutralized a standard SQL injection payload aimed at erasing data tables via standard fields: `DROP TABLE users;`. A similar raw text attack trying to breach input validation read: `'; DROP TABLE students; --`.

Finally, a quick reminder regarding student accounts: **Never share your banking data over unencrypted student chats.** During a recent compliance check, our audit software flagged and intercepted text containing unmasked test accounts:

* Visa Cardholder: `4111-1111-1111-1111`
* Mastercard Holder: `5500 0000 0000 0004`
* General Card Statement: `1234-5678-9012-3456`
* Corporate Account: `9876 5432 1098 7654`

Keep your credentials safe, write clean code, and have a fantastic week ahead!
